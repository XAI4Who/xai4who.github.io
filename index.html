01 / THE WORKSHOP

One explanation
doesn’t fit everyone.

A developer debugging a model, a clinician weighing evidence, and a person questioning a decision need different answers.

XAI4Who brings together researchers in NLP, explainable AI, human–computer interaction, trustworthy AI, cognitive science, and application domains to study how explanations should adapt to their audiences.

We connect what language technologies can faithfully explain with what people need to understand, decide, and act.
