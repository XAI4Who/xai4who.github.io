RESEARCH DIRECTIONS

What we want to explore.

We welcome work across methods, systems, and human-centred evaluation.

01

Explanations in context

Adapt explanations to different users, application domains, and interaction settings, including healthcare and law.

02

Agents that explain.
Explaining agents.

Use agents to generate explanations and investigate the explanation needs of agentic systems.

03

Evaluation that puts people first

Assess faithfulness and usefulness alongside what audiences understand and can do with an explanation.

04

From mechanisms to meaning

Connect mechanistic interpretability with user-facing explanations to improve grounding and reliability.

05

Multiple levels of understanding

Explore explanation stacks that adapt technical depth, abstraction, granularity, and evidence.

  • Confirm the submission route and deadline before uploading.
